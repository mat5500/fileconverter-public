Privacy Policy

File Converter: PDF Maker · Last updated 23 October 2026
Short version: your files never leave your device. File Converter has no account, no analytics and no advertising.

Your files

All conversions run locally on your iPhone, iPad or Mac using Apple's built-in frameworks. Files you open, their names and their contents are never uploaded, shared or seen by us. Converted files are stored only in the app's own folder on your device (also visible in the Files app) until you delete them.

Camera and photos

The camera is used only when you choose Scan to PDF. Photos are read only when you pick them in the system photo picker. Neither leaves your device.

Purchases

The optional Pro upgrade is sold through Apple's App Store. Apple processes the payment; we never receive your payment details. To record and restore purchases we use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc.), which receives an anonymous app user identifier and the App Store transaction details (product, price, date, country). This information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Data we do not collect

No contact details, no location, no contacts, no browsing history, no usage analytics, no advertising identifiers, no crash reports sent to us.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no personal information from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete converted files in the app (Settings › Delete All Converted Files) or delete the app. Purchase records held by Apple and RevenueCat contain no personal content; contact us to ask about them.
