Common questions

Where are my converted files?
In the app's Library tab, and in the Files app under On My iPhone (or iPad) › File Converter.
What does Free include?
One file at a time, up to 25 MB and 10 pages, with all core conversions. For a longer document you can keep the first 10 pages or upgrade.
What does Pro add?
Batch conversion, combining files into one PDF, unlimited pages and size, PDF to Word, merge, split, compress, protect, rotate and PDF to text. It's a one-time purchase, with no subscription.
I bought Pro on another device.
Open Settings › Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.
A Pages, Keynote or Numbers file won't convert on Mac.
On Mac, export it from Pages, Keynote or Numbers as Word, PowerPoint or Excel first, or convert it on iPhone or iPad.
Is anything uploaded?
No. Every conversion runs on your device. See the privacy policy.
